Select Configuration Items in Scope
Every CI listed in the Draft Template SCOPE sheet. Ratios are from the DS (Dedicated Sizing) and G3 benchmark sheets. Shows device:FTE ratio and L1:L2:L3 split.
Step 3 of 7 — Volumetrics
Enter DC & DR Unit Counts
Mirrors the CMS FTE Model formula: DC Units + DR Units = Total Instances. Total Instances ÷ FTE Ratio = Base FTE Required.
